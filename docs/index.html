Inside the market

مارکیت

Afghan staples and international groceries, so your meals at home are complete.

Bakery

نانوایی

Afghan naan, baked fresh every day inside our bakery. Long, golden and covered in seeds.

Halal butcher

قصابی حلال

Fresh halal meat at the counter, cut and cleaned how you like it, and a wide variety of frozen meat as well.

Fresh produce

میوه و ترکاری تازه

Fresh vegetables and fruit, from eggplant and tomatoes to bunches of herbs.

Groceries

مواد غذایی

Rice, spices, tea, dried fruit and nuts, with pantry staples from Afghanistan and around the world. We accept EBT on eligible groceries.

Clothing and hijabs

لباس و حجاب

Men’s and women’s cultural clothing, from peran tumban to abayas, and hijabs in hundreds of colors and styles.

From our kitchen

رستورانت

Our restaurant serves Afghan home cooking, made here every day. Sit down at one of our tables or take it home.

  • Kabob

    کباب

    Cut small the Afghan way and grilled over charcoal, with several skewers to a plate.

  • Qabuli palaw

    قابلی پلو

    Seasoned rice with carrots and raisins, served over a tender lamb shank. Also known as kabuli pulao.

  • Bolani

    بولانی

    Thin stuffed flatbread, fried until crisp.

  • Sheer yakh

    شیریخ

    Afghan ice cream, served by the scoop.
